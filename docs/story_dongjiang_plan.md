# 東江 · The East River — story campaign plan (draft 1)

A fourth story campaign, about 10 hours, set where none of the other three go: the islands off Korea, Korea itself, the
Shandong coast and the sea. 1621–1637, from the fall of Liaoyang to the fall of Pidao. One man of Liaodong grows up in it, from
a smith's boy to an officer, with the same file of men around him for sixteen years: they age, marry, desert, defect and die.

## Why this war

- **Nothing overlaps:** Zhejiang 1619–21, the Divine Engine Division 1629–31 and the Plain White Niru 1619–31 never leave the
  Liaodong mainland and Beijing. The East River is the war's other front: refugees, islands, raids behind the Jin lines, two
  invasions of Korea, a mutiny, and the defection that gave the Qing its guns and its navy.
- **Moral weight:** Mao Wenlong is a hero to some and a crook to others; Yuan Chonghuan executes him. Kong Youde, Geng
  Zhongming and Shang Kexi, all East River men, go over to the Jin. The player's file lives through every side of it.
- **A real ending:** in 1637 the island falls, and the men who take it are East River men who went over in 1633.

## Shape

Six acts and 21 chapters. Chapters 14–18 split on a choice at Wuqiao (go with the mutiny, or not), so each play is 21
chapters and a replay shows the other side of five of them.

## Variety (no two battles the same)

Each battle's main verb is different: escape, hold a ford, open a gate, rearguard, burn and withdraw, beach defence, fortify,
liberate, retreat over ice, hold a mountain fortress, escort, drill, a scripted scene, take a city, sabotage, siege attack,
siege defence, break out, storm, defend a harbour, ambush, landing, last stand.

## Systems to reuse or add

- **Reuse:** mission phases and objectives, the siege system (chapter 16), choices and deeds, camp talk, road scenes, the
  file grid, the soldier group.
- **Add:**
  - a campaign runner (`dongjiang.js`, modelled on jin.js) with its own save;
  - camps on varied maps (beach, farmhouse, wall, ship) instead of always the flat night camp;
  - new maps in sim_maps.js: Liaoyang's west gate, Zhenjiang town, the Pidao beach, the Tieshan palisade, the Yonggol
    fortress, Dengzhou with its water gate, the Lüshun harbour;
  - a ship prop (junk: hull, masts, sails) that can be beached or moored;
  - the file growing old: ages in the cards, a man's look changing with the years (grey hair, beards);
  - promotion: chapters 1–6 as a spearman, 7–11 as a musketeer and file leader, 12 on as an officer leading the file and a
    second squad.

## The file

The player is 李鐵 Li Tie, the smith's son, 17 in 1621. The file:

- 趙大 Zhao Da, a Liaoyang garrison veteran who carries the family out;
- 王二妞 Wang Erniu, Tie's sister (in the refugee column, later married on the island);
- 孫老鐵 Old Sun, the father's smith friend, who carries the chain;
- 金順 Kim Sun, a Korean border boy;
- 錢秀才 Qian, a failed scholar who keeps a diary;
- 馬六 Ma Liu, a fisherman of Pidao;
- 胡三 Hu San, a deserter from the banners (a Han bondservant who ran);
- 吳狗兒 Wu Gou'er, a pickpocket boy;
- 陳把總 Captain Chen, a Mao Wenlong loyalist;
- 鄭老 Old Zheng, a cook and the father figure;
- 劉黑子 Liu Heizi, angry and hungry, who goes with the mutiny on either branch.

Later, 3 or 4 recruits join, and some men leave. Real figures appear as officers and guests: Mao Wenlong, Chen Jisheng, Kong
Youde, Geng Zhongming, Shang Kexi, Huang Long, Shen Shikui, Jeong Bong-su, Yuan Chonghuan, Gonçalo Teixeira.

## Critique of draft 1

1. **21 chapters, each with a camp, is a lot of the same loop** (walk to a man, press E, read, sleep). Over ten hours that is
   what will feel repetitive. Fix: only about half the chapters get a full camp. The rest get a road scene (talk while
   marching), a scripted scene with a choice, or go straight in. Each full camp should have one thing to do besides talking:
   a burial, mending, a wedding, drawing lots, a letter, a trial.
2. **The fork doubles the writing (10 extra missions) while each player sees only half.** That is the most expensive part.
   Fix: keep the fork, but make the a and b versions of a chapter share the map, the boxes and most groups (a mission
   swapped with `side` and its phases flipped), and give each its own scripted beats. Writing cost goes up about 1.4 times,
   not 2.
3. **Battles 1–11 are all small fights with a file; the player may never feel the scale.** Fix: two chapters with real armies
   (10 Yonggol, 16 the Laizhou siege) and, from chapter 12, the player leads a squad with orders.
4. **Historical risk:** some details (the granary raid in 1622, a Jin boat raid on Pidao in 1623) are invented. Fix: label
   them in the cards as composites drawn from the record (Mao's raids are well attested, the specific ones aren't), and keep
   the dated, named events accurate: Zhenjiang 1621, Yongchon, the 1627 invasion, Yonggol, Shuangdao 1629, Wuqiao 1631,
   Dengzhou and Laizhou, Lüshun 1633, Pidao 1637.
5. **Atrocities:** the Liaodong massacres, the mutineers' sack of Shandong towns and the killing of captives are part of the
   story. Fix: show them through what the men see and say, never as a game objective. No mission rewards killing civilians;
   the mutiny branch makes the player live with what his side does.
6. **"Ten hours" can't come from padding.** No timers or waiting: every phase ends when its fighting or task is done (the
   engine already does this). The length has to come from more chapters, more distinct objectives and real camp choices,
   not from longer walks.
7. **A single protagonist over sixteen years can get monotonous.** Fix: chapters jump in time with title cards (a year
   passes); the file changes; one or two chapters are played as someone else (chapter 15b as a Portuguese gunner's
   assistant, or Erniu in chapter 6's beach raid).
8. **Twenty-one battles I can't playtest in a browser here.** Fix: a headless harness that runs every mission's phases with
   the hero moved to each objective, so every chapter is checked to be completable and to end, and each act is published
   as it's done so it can be played and reported on.
9. **Korean and Portuguese characters:** handle them with respect and accuracy, and use their own names and terms (의병
   righteous army, 산성 fortress), not caricature.

## Draft 2 (after the critique)

- **21 chapters, with full camps in 11:** chapters 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16 (a/b), 18 (a/b) and 20. The other ten
  open on a road scene or a scripted scene, or go straight in.
- **The fork:** a and b missions share a map and data; there are about 26 missions in all.
- **Playable others:** in chapter 6 you play Erniu, defending the fishing village while the men are away; in chapter 15b you
  play Tie inside Dengzhou with Teixeira's gunners.
- **Things to do in camp:** a burial (2), counting the money (12), a wedding (6), drawing lots (8), a letter home (14),
  shaving the head or not (18a), and others.
- **Build order:** each act is published as it's done (I → II → III → IV → V → VI), with the harness run on every mission.
