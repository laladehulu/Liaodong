明清戰爭

Ming Dynasty · 1619

The Ming Dynasty against the rising Manchu Later Jin. Build an army squad by squad, ride in at its head, fight with dao and rifle in first person, or climb to a bird's-eye view and give orders. Deterministic lockstep keeps every peer's battle identical.

Sound: instrument recordings from the Versilian Community Sample Library and VSCO-2 Community Edition (CC0, Versilian Studios); erhu and suona are physical models written for this game; battle effects and the war-drum recordings and the Han-civilization pieces “Red Dawn” and “Hill of Sorrows” (music by Omri Lahav) from 0 A.D. © Wildfire Games, CC BY-SA 3.0, trimmed and re-encoded. Soldiers' voices: cries of pain and death from Warfork and the “80 CC0 creature SFX” packs via the CC0 Public Domain Sounds collection (CC0).

Hold the ringed objectives to score: first side to 300 points, or the last side standing, wins. Each squad's captain carries its banner; if he falls the squad wavers. Shellfire and fire arrows set dry grass and houses alight, and the wind drives the flames. At Yangzhou the rain fouls muzzle-loaders and lightning seeks the banners.

Blocks must match the attack: an overhead needs an up block, a thrust a down block, and swings are blocked on the side they come from. A block raised in the last fifth of a second is a perfect parry that stuns the attacker and gives you a fast, heavy counter.
